\chapter{Ethical Considerations}\label{chapter:ethical-considerations}

Along the scientific research about nazi symbol image classification task, there are some ethical considerations we
need to pay attention.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Handling Sensitive Content:} Nazi symbols are associated with hate, violence, and genocide. Any
  dataset containing such symbols must be handled with great care to avoid exposure or normalization of hateful
  imagery. Strict access controls and disclaimers should be in place when working with or sharing these datasets.

  \item \textbf{Purpose and Intent:} Clearly state that the aim of the project is to aid in the identification and
  moderation of harmful content to prevent the spread of hate symbols, not to support or amplify them in any way.
  Transparency in the project's objective is essential to avoid misinterpretation or misuse.

  \item \textbf{Legal Compliance:} Many countries (e.g., Germany, Austria) have laws restricting the display and
  distribution of Nazi imagery. Ensure compliance with all applicable laws when collecting, processing, or displaying
  such images in the thesis or during research.

  \item \textbf{Data Sourcing and Annotation:} Ensure that all data used in the project is sourced from publicly
  available and legally permissible datasets. If human annotation is involved, take care to minimize annotators'
  exposure to harmful content and provide mental health resources or warnings as needed.

  \item \textbf{Bias and Misclassification:} Models may misclassify benign imagery as containing Nazi symbols, or fail
  to detect them in modified forms. Such errors can lead to false accusations or missed detection of harmful content.
  Measures to mitigate bias and improve robustness (e.g., through diverse and balanced training data) must be taken
  seriously.

  \item \textbf{Dual-Use Risk:} A system capable of identifying Nazi symbols could potentially be misused to automate
  the creation of undetectable variants or to track individuals for malicious purposes. This dual-use potential should
  be acknowledged and addressed through limitations on deployment or sharing of code and models.

  \item \textbf{Privacy and Consent:} If any datasets contain identifiable individuals (e.g., people wearing symbols),
  care must be taken to anonymize data and respect privacy rights. Consent and ethical clearance are necessary for
  datasets containing personally identifiable information.

  \item \textbf{Transparency and Accountability:} Document the development process, dataset composition, and model
  limitations clearly in the thesis. Encourage responsible use and provide recommendations for future researchers on
  how to avoid misuse of such classification systems.
\end{itemize}
